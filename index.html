Meet BTS

Read the story. Tap the purple words to find them in the Word Bank.

BTS is a famous music group from South Korea. There are seven members: RM, Jin, SUGA, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jungkook. They sing, rap and dance.

Their lyrics are often emotional. They sing about friendship, love, stress and hope. Many songs are comforting when you feel sad or tired.

Their choreography is powerful and full of energy. The members practice a lot. Teamwork is very important for them.

BTS debuted on June 13, 2013. Now they have millions of fans around the world. The fans are called ARMY. BTS is grateful to ARMY, and ARMY is proud of BTS.

Their message is simple: “Love yourself.” Their journey is inspiring.

Look and think

  • Who are the members?
  • What do the lyrics talk about?
  • How do you feel when you listen to BTS?
  • Why is their message inspiring?

Teacher's Corner

How to use this page (A1–A2 level)

1. Read aloud together. Ask the student to find and click the purple words. 2. Flip the Word Bank cards and ask for a new sentence with each word. 3. Use “Let's Talk” for speaking. Each card has a tag (Wh- question, Would, Do you, Feelings). Ask follow-ups: Why? When? How did you feel? 4. Finish with the quiz and the opinion sentences.

Grammar and feelings focus

Wh- questions: Where, Who, What, Why, When, How. Conditional: Would you like…? I would feel… / I wouldn't…. Feelings: happy, sad, excited, nervous, calm, proud, grateful, hopeful, motivated, emotional. Practice: I feel… when… / It makes me feel…

Extra speaking ideas

Choose a song and describe how it makes you feel. Compare a happy song and a sad song. Ask your teacher: “What music makes you feel calm?” Practise third person: He sings… She feels… They dance…
